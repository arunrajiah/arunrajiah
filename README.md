# Hi, I'm Arun

I build **open-source AI and web tools for the people mainstream tech tends to skip** — conservationists, non-profits, small publishers, and public-interest teams. Based in Chennai, India. Founder of **Code 4 Cause** and Managing Partner at [Lvl1 Accelerator](https://lvl1accelerator.com).

**Everything I ship is free and self-hostable** — if it helps you or the people you serve, [**sponsor the work →**](https://github.com/sponsors/arunrajiah).

## Featured projects

### [odoopilot](https://github.com/arunrajiah/odoopilot) ![stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/arunrajiah/odoopilot?style=flat&label=%E2%98%85)
Your team uses Odoo — without logging into Odoo. A free, self-hosted AI assistant on Telegram & WhatsApp: apply for leave, approve requests, update CRM, validate stock — by chat, in 15 languages. For small teams and non-profits on Odoo Community.

### [birdecho](https://github.com/arunrajiah/birdecho) ![stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/arunrajiah/birdecho?style=flat&label=%E2%98%85)
Your backyard bird station, on your phone — a companion app for BirdNET-Pi, BirdNET-Go, and BirdWeather. For citizen scientists, birders, and conservation programs.

### [cctvql](https://github.com/arunrajiah/cctvql) ![stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/arunrajiah/cctvql?style=flat&label=%E2%98%85)
Ask your cameras questions in plain English instead of scrubbing hours of footage. Works with Frigate and ONVIF. For conservation groups, schools, and homelabbers.

### [speciesnet-studio](https://github.com/arunrajiah/speciesnet-studio) ![stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/arunrajiah/speciesnet-studio?style=flat&label=%E2%98%85)
Self-hosted review UI for SpeciesNet wildlife-classifier predictions. Triage thousands of camera-trap detections without sending data to the cloud.

### [openmem](https://github.com/arunrajiah/openmem) ![stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/arunrajiah/openmem?style=flat&label=%E2%98%85)
Local-first, private memory for Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini. Your AI history stays on your machine. For journalists, lawyers, clinicians, and the privacy-minded.

### [arunai-auto-news-poster](https://github.com/arunrajiah/arunai-auto-news-poster) ![stars](https://img.shields.io/github/stars/arunrajiah/arunai-auto-news-poster?style=flat&label=%E2%98%85)
WordPress plugin that drafts posts from RSS feeds via OpenAI/Anthropic. For local news sites and niche publications that can't staff full-time editors.

**Also building:** [frostwatch](https://github.com/arunrajiah/frostwatch) (Snowflake cost observability) · [supafleet](https://github.com/arunrajiah/supafleet) (self-host multiple Supabase projects) · [coworker](https://github.com/arunrajiah/coworker) (memory-first AI coworker for founders).
